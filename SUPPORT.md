# Aria support

Need help with Aria? Email **studentoclockapp@gmail.com** or open an issue at
https://github.com/theprojectmaker/aria-ios/issues

## Common questions

**The first launch is downloading something. Why?**
Aria runs its AI model on your device, so the model has to be downloaded once
(about 1.5 GB for the default). Use Wi-Fi. After that Aria works offline.

**How do I get a smarter (or faster) model?**
Open the menu, then Models. Download any model in the list. New chats use the
smartest model on your device automatically, or you can lock a chat to one model
from the model button at the top of the chat.

**A model says it did not fit in memory.**
Larger models need a device with 6 GB of memory or more. Aria remembers and uses a
smaller model instead. You can delete the large one on the Models page to free space.

**How do I free up storage?**
Menu, then Models, then Delete next to any model you no longer want.

**Can Aria read a worksheet or a page?**
Yes. Tap the camera or the + button, take or choose a photo, and ask your question.
Clear, well-lit photos of printed text work best.

**Is my data sent anywhere?**
No. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Are the answers always right?**
No. Aria's answers come from AI models and can be wrong. Arithmetic is checked by a
built-in calculator, but check anything important with a reliable source.
