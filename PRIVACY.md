# Aria privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Aria is an AI assistant that runs on your iPhone or iPad. This page explains what
happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Aria does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising,
no analytics and no tracking. Your chats, photos and notes are processed on your
device and are stored only on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Chats and notes.** Everything you type and every answer is saved only in the
  app's storage on your device. Deleting a note, or deleting the app, removes it.
- **Photos.** When you attach or take a photo, the text in it is read on your
  device. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
- **AI models.** The models run on your device's own processor. Your questions are
  never sent to a server to be answered.
- **Settings.** Your theme and model choices are stored on your device.

## When Aria uses the internet

Aria works offline once it is set up. It connects to the internet only to download
files to your device:

- **AI models** are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time
  you use them, or when you choose one on the Models page.
- **The offline Library** (encyclopedia and dictionary) is downloaded from GitHub
  (github.com) if you choose to add it.
- **App updates.** Aria may check Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer
  version of the app's code.

These are ordinary file downloads. They do not include your chats, photos or any
personal information. Like any download, the service that hosts the file can see
your IP address, as described in that service's own privacy policy.

## Voice input

If you tap the microphone, Aria uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your
speech into text. Aria asks for on-device recognition when your device supports
it. On devices that do not, Apple may process the audio on its servers under
Apple's privacy policy. Aria does not record, keep or send your audio anywhere
itself. Voice input is optional and needs your permission.

## Permissions

- **Camera** and **Photos**: only when you choose to take or attach a photo.
- **Microphone** and **Speech Recognition**: only when you use voice input.

You can change these at any time in the Settings app.

## Children

Aria does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## AI answers

Aria's answers are generated by AI models and can be wrong. Check anything
important with a reliable source.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
